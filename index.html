Laser hair removal, the Blossom way.

A concentrated beam of light is absorbed by the pigment in the hair shaft, heating the follicle so regrowth is inhibited. Expect a 10–25% reduction after the first session; most clients need 8–12 sessions, then one or two a year for maintenance.

  • 1Don't miss a session — consistency is what delivers results.
  • 2Avoid tweezing or waxing any area undergoing laser treatment.
  • 3Shave the day before, and limit sun exposure before and during your course.

Cancellation policy

Appointments cancelled within 24 hours of the scheduled time are non-refundable and non-redeemable. This means:

  • No refunds will be issued under any circumstance.
  • No credit or rescheduling is offered for future appointments.
  • Payment for the missed or cancelled session can't be transferred, reused or applied to another booking.

To reschedule, please do so at least 24 hours before your appointment. We enforce this to respect the time reserved exclusively for you, and to be fair to all clients.
